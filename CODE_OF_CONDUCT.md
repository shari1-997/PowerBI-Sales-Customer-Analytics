# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We aim to keep project discussions respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Provide actionable, relevant feedback.
- Accept constructive criticism professionally.
- Respect privacy and do not share confidential data.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions.

## Reporting
Please contact the project owner through the contact method listed on the repository profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in public issues.
